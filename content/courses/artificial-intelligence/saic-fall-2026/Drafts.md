*Slop-a-thon*

For this assignment, create your own unique brand of "slop."

***"Slop is the media that attention networks prompt humans to make"** - Mat Dryhurst,*

What do I mean when I say "slop"? You know it when you see it. Fruit Love Island. Cats cooking meals. Old Internet memes come to life. "Slop" is not necessarily a derogatory term. Rather, it describes a certain type of content—there can be high and low quality slop (in my opinion).

For this assignment. I want you to hone in on some strange, unique aesthetic territory. Settle down in your own quiet corner of the latent space, and make videos that are all variations on a similar theme, subject, or scenario.

For example, this [compilation of a cat loudly playing various instruments on someone's porch in the middle of the night](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/cJsoNQcu2YM). Or, the [Romanian watermelon church](https://www.are.na/block/50308308), or the [Apple Dog trend](https://youtu.be/Z43M0rDV6YU)

In the cat instrument example, everything is more or less the same between each video, except for the instrument the cat plays. In the watermelon church, the scenes depict different scenarios, but the overall vibe, as well as key subject matter, remains consistent. Apple Dog begins from the exact same image and becomes something else in each video.

All of the above are valid approaches to this assignment.

### Phase 1 - Finding your corner of the latent space
First, start with images. Find a scenario, a vibe, a subject matter, through image prompting. Use any model you'd like (from SD 1.5 up to newer models like Krea 2 or Flux 2). Feel free to use any variations on these models. You don't even have to AI generate your images! You can use real images found in the world, or images that you capture.

### Phase 2 - variations on a theme (generate generate generate!)
Once you've found your theme/idea, start generating some videos with LTX 2.5. This is where you'll spend most of your time. Generate a bunch of variations, try all sorts of things. Try different prompts, different video lengths, different aspect ratios, different starting images. Try Image-to-video first, then try FLF2V (first-last-frame to video) if you want.

You're allowed to experiment with other local models (MiniMax H3, Wan2.2, LTX 2.3, etc.), but please keep it to these smaller models, rather than using the frontier models you have to pay for. (For future projects, if you wish to continue using video, you are free to use whatever tools you like)

### Phase 3 - Curating
After generating a large body of videos, select your 10 favorites. Upload them to a shared Google Drive folder (I'll create that and link to that soon).

Next week, these will be combined into a collective feed showcasing all of our videos.

### Guidelines/words of caution

These models are strange, unpredictable, and can be a mirror or an amplifier of some of our darker cultural artifacts. Although you are more or less free to create what you want for this assignment, I have a few guard rails I would like to impose:
1. Avoid generating people from popular culture, or public figures. We've all seen plenty of Donald Trump and Elon Musk. We're just fine without more images of them, thank you very much.
2. Be mindful of content that might be considered racist, sexist, homophobic, ableist, etc. Yes, the models are capable of many things, and yes, it is our job to expose the biases of these models. But for the context of a "slop" assignment, be intentional with your outputs. This assignment is not a license to generate whatever you want. **This is still a classroom and a community**
3. Avoid overly sexual or violent imagery. A lot of AI imagery is inherently horrifying, so some horror is okay!
